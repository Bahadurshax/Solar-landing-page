## Design Specificity Verdict

Content layer is unmistakably this product; structural layer is a scroll template that survives a content swap.

Specific: district/crew copy, dust storms and 45C summers, ten single-event testimonials, georeferenced region map (two real anchors, true lat/lon), About light cycle ordered so the guarantee lands at dusk.

Interchangeable: pinned four-card stack with rail; pinned horizontal pan of three claim panels; map + filter pills + featured card + carousel + stats bar; marquee testimonial wall; footer pushing up with cropped wordmark.

Costliest interchangeable choice is the hero. The one specific move available to a solar installer is the sun. The toggle is 400x70, glass-blurred, two hand-tuned springs, ~40 lines of damping commentary — the largest object in the first viewport — and pressing it swaps a photograph. Both halves render "$0 for Electricity" (hardcoded literal inside the map()). The actual sale is a 52px corner button that does nothing.

Deterministic scan: detect.mjs returned 0 findings, exit 0, on src and index.html. This is NOT a clean bill of health — the detector self-reported DEGRADED (htmlparser2, css-select, css-tree, domutils unavailable), regex fallback only; custom properties, selector matching and computed contrast were never evaluated. Verified not silently broken: registry loads 59 rules; direct regex-engine call also returned 0; control test with a synthetic bad stylesheet correctly fired side-tab, overused-font, gradient-text, bounce-easing.

Visual overlays: NONE. No browser automation exposed (Chrome extension not set up; no MCP browser tool; Playwright/Puppeteer absent). No dev server or live server was started. Both assessments are source-only; live/computed-style rules are unmeasured.

Convergence: both agents independently found the dead CTA. Verified: no id="quote" anywhere in src/; zero form/input/textarea/select elements in the tree.
Detector-side catch the design pass missed: 0 of 6 images declare width/height; ProcessCard.jsx:20 has no loading attribute.

## Overall Impression

Genuine authorship in the writing and one section of real conceptual courage, sitting on top of a conversion funnel that terminates in a wall. The biggest opportunity is finishing the sentence the page already started: ~3,500 lines of CSS carry a visitor to peak conviction at About panel 03, then hand them a "Scroll to top" button.

## What's Working

1. About makes the medium the argument, and proves it by how it degrades — the narrow fallback drops pin, pan and sun but keeps the light cycle.
2. Testimonials do work the company's own voice cannot — ten single-event quotes, no ornament; "My father asked what the catch was. Four years on he is still looking for it."
3. The map is georeferenced, not decorated — two real anchors, true district lat/lon, no pin placed by eye.

## Persona Red Flags

Jordan (first-timer): presses the toggle (biggest thing on screen, both halves labelled identically), gets a photo swap, believes that is the main interaction. Presses the CTA — nothing, twice. Clicks a nav link, thrown to top. Never reaches the footer, which holds the only phone number.

Riley (stress tester): wants guarantee terms. "Learn more" on process card 04 — the exact claim under test — has a full hover state and no handler. All four dead. Footer Terms href="#" throws him to top, costing the scroll he paid for through two pinned sections. Notices $18,420 USD against som billing, and 5,200+ homes since 2019 = ~2 installs per working day for seven years.

Casey (mobile): taps Get Quote, nothing, assumes loading. Aims at the Nukus pin, hits Chimboy — at 375px the pin centres compute ~31px apart while .region-map__pin is 46x46. The featured card silently swaps. The footer's one filled control is "Scroll to top". The burger CTA points at nonexistent #quote.

## Minor Observations

- Two brand marks: lucide Zap bolt in header, hand-drawn eight-ray sun in footer. The sun is better and intentional.
- No :focus-visible on navbar/footer controls; .about, .our-cases and .toggle__option all define one, so the pattern was known.
- ModeToggle disables the focused button for up to 1200ms; focus drops to body in several browsers.
- 0 of 6 images declare width/height. ProcessCard.jsx:20 has no loading attribute.
- Testimonial wall renders no heading element (it carries aria-label, so it is not nameless).
- No meta description, no Open Graph tags. Title is good.
- Reduced-motion handling is unusually thorough; better than most production sites.
- Detector false positives: a naive "<h1" grep misses <motion.h1> — the outline is correct, one h1, no skipped levels. RegionMap alt="" is paired with aria-hidden="true", which is correct practice.

## Questions to Consider

1. The guarantee is the product. Why is the actual contract clause not on the page?
2. Who is this page in? English copy, Nukus audience, Karakalpak/Russian working languages, USD savings, no language switcher.
3. Your testimonials say the likeliest converter already heard about you from a neighbour. What does this page look like designed for the convinced visitor first?
4. About proves you can make time the argument. What would make seven years something a visitor experiences?
5. Every "Learn more" is dead. What was supposed to be behind them?
